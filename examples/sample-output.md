## 1. LinkedIn - Mon 14 Apr - receipts
**Hook options:**
1. We spent 11 months building a feature four people used.
2. Four accounts used it. We had 9,000.
3. The feature every customer asked for is the one nobody opened.

**Post / Script:**
We spent 11 months building a feature four people used.

Four. Out of 9,000 accounts.

Nobody lied to us. Every customer call said they wanted it, and every one of them meant it at the time, which is the part that took me two years to understand.

People describe the job they wish they had. They pay for the job they've actually got.

So we changed one question. Instead of asking what you need, we ask what you did last Tuesday. Boring question. It hasn't been wrong yet.

What's the smallest signal that told you a feature was dead?

## 2. X thread - Wed 16 Apr - teardown
**Hook options:**
1. Every failing project I've seen had the same status update.
2. "Slight delay, nothing blocking." That update means the project is already gone.

**Post / Script:**
1/ Every failing project I've seen had the same status update.

2/ "Slight delay on our side, nothing blocking."

3/ Nothing blocking is the tell. Real work has friction. A week with no friction means nobody touched it.

4/ Healthy projects report problems. Dead ones report progress.

5/ The fix isn't a better template. Ask what specifically moved this week, and who watched it move.

6/ If the answer takes longer than one sentence, it didn't move.

**Notes:** Post 1 works alone if the thread underperforms. No CTA by design.
